# Compliance and Product Language

## Product Positioning

TradeSentinel is a research and educational market-intelligence platform during the MVP phase.

Before public or paid deployment, obtain professional legal review for applicable securities research, advisory, advertising, and data-licensing obligations.

## Allowed Language

- "Current evidence suggests a moderately bullish outlook."
- "The model estimates a 61% probability of a positive return over twenty trading days."
- "This stock may be worth further research."
- "The available evidence is mixed."

## Disallowed Language

- "This stock will definitely rise."
- "Buy this immediately."
- "Guaranteed profit."
- "You cannot lose money."

## Prediction Presentation

Every prediction must show:

- Horizon
- Data cutoff
- Probability distribution
- Confidence
- Model version
- Risks
- Limitations

## Performance Reporting

- Do not publish model accuracy without a sufficient sample.
- Show methodology and evaluation window.
- Distinguish backtest, paper test, and live results.
- Do not cherry-pick predictions.
